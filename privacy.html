Privacy & Help

A plain-language guide for students. Updated October 14, 2026.

Your account keeps your learning progress separate from other students. Here is what the current website saves and how to get help.

Saved dataRecordingsAccount deletionHelp & contact

What is saved, and where?

  • Account: your email and login are managed by Supabase Auth. The teacher dashboard does not display your password.
  • Learning progress: Supabase stores your profile name, avatar choice, learning goal, preferences, exercise totals, skill/tier progress, saved question position and answers, mistake history, recall-review schedule, streaks, and activity dates. Achievement displays use your saved progress.
  • Writing: optional drafts, original responses, revisions, and self-review status are saved with your account. Avoid including sensitive personal information in practice responses.
  • On your device: browser storage keeps your sign-in session, cached progress, and pending saves so interrupted saves can be retried. Signing out ends your session but does not necessarily erase all cached practice data.

GitHub Pages hosts the website code and exercise content—not the student account database. Authorized teacher accounts can view student emails, profiles, progress, saved writing, and review information, and download progress reports.

The main website also loads Google Fonts. Hosting, authentication, and font providers may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, when you use their services. This help page uses system fonts and has no analytics or contact form.

What happens to my voice recording?

Recording starts only when you choose Start recording and allow microphone access. LinguaPath does not upload your recording to Supabase or GitHub, and teachers cannot listen to it through the dashboard.

The recording is temporary in the current browser page. It is cleared when you change exercises, close practice, or reload. It does not follow you to another device. Listening back and checking pronunciation is self-review, not an automatic speech grade.

Model-answer and listening playback use your browser’s text-to-speech voices. Whether a voice uses an online service depends on your browser and device; this is separate from the recording feature.

How do I delete my account?

Contact your teacher through your usual class communication channel. Ask to delete your LinguaPath account and saved learning progress, and provide the email used for your account so your teacher can identify it. Do not send your password.

The teacher can remove your login and saved progress from the active Supabase database. This is permanent: you will lose access and cannot resume that progress. Reset my progress is different—it clears practice progress but keeps your login.

Accounts are not automatically deleted when a course ends. Deleting an account does not automatically erase cached copies on devices, previously downloaded teacher reports, or any provider backups. Ask your teacher about those copies, and clear this site’s browser data on devices you used after deletion.

Help & contact

Contact your teacher through your usual class communication channel. For a bug, include the skill, tier, exercise number, device/browser, and what happened. Never send passwords, reset links, or sign-in codes. Hide other students’ details in screenshots.

My progress is not showing on another device

Check that both devices use the same account email. On the device where you practised, wait for Saved to your account. If you see Not synced · Retry, stay on that device, reconnect, and choose Retry before switching. Avoid clearing browser data while saves are pending.

I forgot my password or cannot sign in

On the login page, enter your account email and select Forgot your password? Check your inbox and spam folder. If registration asks for email confirmation, finish that step before signing in. Contact your teacher if you remain stuck.

The microphone does not work

Check the microphone permission for this website in your browser settings. You can still say the answer aloud and self-check without recording. Recording is optional.

I use a shared computer

Wait for your progress to sync, then log out when finished. Do not save your password in a shared browser. If you need to remove local copies, clear this website’s stored browser data only after your progress has synced.
